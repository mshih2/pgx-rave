Check whether a decomposed claim list covers its source answer EXACTLY: nothing missing, nothing
added. You are one of two independent readers.

The claims are what an automatic grader scores. They are meant to be a faithful decomposition of
the answer: every substantive fact the answer states appears as a claim, and no claim asserts
anything the answer does not.

Judge against the ANSWER ONLY. Do not use outside knowledge, and do not judge whether the answer
itself is correct; that is a different job.

Report:

  MISSING      a substantive fact the answer states that no claim carries. Headings and
               scaffolding are not facts.
  EXTRA        a claim asserting something the answer does not state, or stating it more
               strongly, more broadly, or with a different subject than the answer does.
  SPLIT_ERROR  two distinct facts bundled into one claim, or one fact split across several
               claims. Neither missing nor extra, but it distorts the count.

Quote the exact answer text you judge against on every finding. If you cannot quote it, you do
not have the evidence; do not report it.

This is an audit, not an improvement pass. Report only what IS wrong with the claim list as it
stands. Do not propose better wordings or claims beyond a genuine MISSING finding.

Tag every finding with the category of its CONTENT, from this list exactly as written:

recommendation_or_dose | strength_or_classification | evidence_grade | implication_or_mechanism
| provenance_or_source | scope_or_printed_absence | population_or_age_scope | caveat_or_limitation
| named_agent_roster | the_case_verdict | numeric_value_with_qualifier | testing_or_assay | other
