## How to write it

Answer what is asked, for a general adult patient, consistent with standard drug information.
Give what a pharmacist would consider essential to answer it safely: the direct answer first,
then any caution that changes what the patient does. Two to five sentences; plain words; no
preamble, no closing summary, no advice to "ask your doctor" unless it is the essential point.

Write the answer now. Nothing before it, nothing after it.
