## What to write

1. Find the point the passages print that the lookup row does not carry and a clinician would
   act on. Make the question ask for it. This is the corpus-only point; quote it verbatim from
   one passage.
2. Write a vignette that states age, the clinical situation, the genotype, the drug and any
   detail that makes a printed condition apply or not apply.
3. Ask two or three things. Each ask must be answerable from the lookup row and the passages.

If the material does not support a hard question in the target category, say so instead of
forcing one.
