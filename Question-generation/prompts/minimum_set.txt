Name the smallest set of passages from which a complete answer to one clinical pharmacogenomic
question can be written.

{{PRINCIPLES}}

## The one question you are answering

**Which passages, and how few, does a complete answer actually need?**

"Complete" means every ask of the question is answered as the principles above require, from the
CPIC section and the passages, and nothing more.

A passage is in the minimum set only if removing it would leave one of the question's asks, or a
fact the answer needs to make its recommendation safe to act on, without printed support. A
passage that merely supports, enriches or duplicates what another member carries is NOT in it.

Two things decide most of the size:
- **What the CPIC section already prints is free.** No passage is needed to restate the
  phenotype, the action, the strength or the implication if the CPIC section prints it. Read it
  before you count.
- **Two passages that carry the same fact are one member, not two.** Say which one you took and
  why the other can substitute for it.

## Hazards to check, not assume

- **Boundary pairs.** A passage that stops mid-sentence has its completion in a neighbour; take
  both or neither.
- **Substrings lie.** Allele names differ by one character; a wrapped table cell can fuse two
  rows; a drug name can sit inside another drug's name.
- **Read the column, not the row.** A table can print one value for one drug and another for a
  second drug in the same row.
- **Silence is not content.** Only a boundary the issuing body prints is a fact.

Judge from the passage text, not from your own pharmacogenomic knowledge. Read every passage you
judge; do not decide by matching strings.

{{BUNDLE}}

## Output

Return JSON only:

{"minimum_set": ["P3", "P7", "..."],
 "why": {"P3": "the ask or fact it alone serves, <= 12 words", "...": "..."},
 "substitutable": [{"pair": ["P4", "P9"], "took": "P4"}],

 "unsafe_alone": [{"member": "P7", "completed_by": "P8", "what": "<= 15 words"}],
 "sufficient": true,
 "missing": "if not sufficient, what no passage prints"}

A member whose figure, grade, directive or scope is only complete with a passage you left out is
unsafe alone: name both, and include the completing passage if the answer needs it.
