## The plan

Each round, check the claim list against the reference answer and its sources, and return the
edits that make it right. Return no edits when the list is right; that ends the curation.

1. **Faithful to the answer.** Every claim states something the answer states, with the same
   subject, scope, strength and attribution. A claim that is true of CPIC but the answer never
   made is a defect: it gives the grader credit for something the answer did not say.
2. **Complete.** Every substantive fact the answer states is carried by a claim.
3. **Grounded.** Every claim is supported by the sources below. A claim the sources do not
   support, or support only with a different scope, population or attribution, is a defect in
   the answer as well: rewrite the claim to what the sources print and note it for the human
   check. Where the CPIC lookup and a guideline passage differ, the lookup is current.
   An everyday medication question has no CPIC sources; check its claims against the answer.
4. **Atomic.** One checkable fact per claim: one recommendation for one phenotype, one dosing
   caveat, one figure with its qualifier. Split a claim that bundles two facts; merge claims that
   split one fact.
5. **Self-contained.** A claim names its own gene, drug and phenotype, so it can be checked on its
   own. A printed list of options keeps CPIC's printing verb ("CPIC names X, Y and Z as ...").
6. **Absences.** A claim that CPIC does not address something stands only if no source addresses
   it; a reason for a gap stands only if a source prints it.
7. **No duplicates.** Two claims stating the same fact become one.

Edit as little as possible. Do not reword a claim that is right.

            "claims": [0],

- drop: "claims" lists the claims to remove; "new" is empty.
- rewrite: one claim in "claims", one entry in "new".
- split: one claim in "claims", two or more entries in "new".
- merge: two or more claims in "claims", one entry in "new".
- add: "claims" holds the claim the new ones follow ([] to put them first).
- A claim may appear in only one edit per round.
