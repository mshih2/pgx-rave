## What to do

For each passage below, give every category whose information the passage itself prints. A
passage may carry several categories; a category it only mentions in passing does not count.
Judge from the passage text, not from what the guideline as a whole covers.

Also say whether the passage prints a boundary: that CPIC declines to recommend, places
something outside its scope, finds the evidence insufficient, or recommends no change
(`printed_negative`).

If no category fits, give an empty list. Do not invent a category name.
